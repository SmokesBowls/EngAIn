===================================
SECTION 3 — SYSTEM CONTRACT (AP v1)
===================================

The AP v1 system promises:

1. **Deterministic Core**
   All rule evaluation, conflict resolution, and predicate
   decisions must be deterministic under fixed seed + context.

2. **Complete Observability**
   Every rule, predicate, conflict, hazard, and effect MUST be
   introspectable via Query API and ZON output.

3. **Transactional Mutation**
   No AP rule may change without:
      (1) snapshot
      (2) patch apply
      (3) validation
      (4) commit or rollback

4. **Domain Segregation**
   Rules tagged for narrative, combat, dream, or economy may
   only affect resources within their domain unless explicitly
   whitelisted.

5. **Human Override Layer**
   Structural and systemic patches MUST require human approval.

6. **Long-Horizon Stability**
   Priority drift, entropy drift, and rule starvation MUST be
   monitored and corrected by Meta-Profile + Design Critic.

7. **Reproducibility**
   All scenario runs must be reproducible under fixed seed.

8. **Safety First**
   Predictive sandbox MUST guarantee:
      - zero side effects
      - zero live mutation
      - complete rollback
