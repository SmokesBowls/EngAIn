ACT 1: THE AFTERMATH OF TRUTH

The Akashic truth settled into Geralt’s bones like a cold, heavy ore. He stood from the library floor, Kyh’s shadowy hands steadying him. The grand, cosmic horror of the Queen’s work—rewriting reality itself from the corpse of a god—should have crushed him. Instead, a grim, focused clarity took hold.

Causal Inertia, he thought, the term from Kaelen’s lessons now a lifeline. Assign the weight.

He marked the Queen’s ambition as inelastic. A monstrous, fixed fact. He marked his own resolve beside it. Another immovable object. The conflict was set; the scale was cosmic, but the principle was brutally simple. Two truths could not occupy the same space. One would break.

“The library showed you the war,” Kyh observed, her voice hushed. “Not the battle.”

“It showed me the battlefield,” Geralt corrected, his voice rough. “And that we are insects upon it. We need to become a force it cannot ignore.”

There was only one place to forge that kind of strength.
