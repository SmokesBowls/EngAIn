scene 015.5 — departure and legacy

scene meta:
location: Orbital Observation Aperture (Crimson Citadel)
time: year 10,550, month 6, day 21
participants: Queen Eduhauana
focus: Queen Eduhauana conducts a final tactical assessment of the Second Coming, notes the elimination of entry-level biological assets, confirms Ash Giant collaboration, outlines long-term projections for the Third, Fourth, and Fifth Comings, and authorizes orbital departure.
continuity: 4 months, 7 days after the discovery of betrayal in scene 015.4
presentation: cutscene
cutscene purpose: Queen Eduhauana's orbital assessment of the Second Coming and the departure of the Crimson Citadel.

Queen Eduhauana stood before the observation aperture one final time, reviewing the Second Coming's outcomes with satisfaction that three thousand years of patience had earned.

The Nephilim manifestation had been unexpected—evidence that the Aeon Keepers maintained more influence than her initial assessments suggested. But the convergence itself had served her purposes perfectly. The ritual's success had fractured Giant unity more completely than military conquest ever could.

Tactical assessment:
- Gruulith, Dregan forces: eliminated by Nephilim countermeasures. Acceptable losses—they were entry-level assets designed for pressure application, not sustained occupation.
- Brajor: eliminated despite defection attempt. Demonstrates consequences of choosing resistance over cooperation. Perfect object lesson for future deployments.
- Ash Giant collaboration secured: 23% of mountain territories now under cooperative administration. Intelligence gathering operational.
- Remaining tribes: fragmented, isolated, mutual trust degraded beyond near-term recovery. Unity impossible without generational healing that occupation will prevent.

Long-term projections:
- Third Coming (in 3,500 cycles): Deploy ice-age protocols, test whether environmental catastrophe prompts cooperation or accelerates fragmentation. Probability matrices suggest fragmentation.
- Fourth Coming (7,000 cycles hence): Introduce human populations as intermediate management layer. Giants' pride will prevent cooperation with "lesser" species, ensuring continued isolation.
- Fifth Coming (10,500 cycles total): Complete network activation. Harvest fully matured bloodline assets (if Tran/Viên genetic lines develop as projected). Indigenous resistance capability: minimal, due to self-imposed fragmentation sustained across four occupation cycles.

"Departure authorization," Eduhauana commanded. "Withdraw primary forces. Maintain Ash Giant cooperation through continued resource provision. Deploy Reptilian garrison forces to monitor remaining tribes. Estimated return: Third Coming configuration."

The Crimson Citadel began its slow ascent toward departure trajectory. Behind them, the planet turned—blue and green and beautiful, its indigenous populations now divided beyond any hope of unity, their resistance broken not through military defeat but through patient exploitation of their own capacity for mistrust.

The Second Coming was complete.

The Sundering was permanent.

And in the cold mathematics of cosmic conquest, this victory was more absolute than any battlefield triumph could achieve.
