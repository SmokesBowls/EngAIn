scene 015.6 — what remains

cutscene purpose: Torhh and Pazuzu's pledge to preserve history and friendship in the scarred valley, concluding Book 3.

Pazuzu and Torhh stood where the ritual had occurred, the valley still scarred by convergence forces that had reshaped terrain at fundamental levels. Around them, evidence of what had been sacrificed: stone fused into glass, water channels frozen then shattered, atmospheric disturbances that made the air itself taste wrong.

"We failed," Torhh said simply, its ancient voice carrying defeat that three thousand years of resistance had never imposed.

"We tried," Pazuzu corrected. "We recognized the lies. We attempted cooperation despite programming designed to prevent it. We summoned the old defenses and paid the price cosmic law demanded. The failure wasn't in the attempt—it was in thinking that attempt would be sufficient against forces that calculate across geological time."

"Then what do we do for the next three thousand years? Watch the tribes isolate further? See the Ash Giants become exactly what the golden ones needed them to be? Wait for the Third Coming to test whether we've learned anything from this disaster?"

"We maintain friendship," Pazuzu replied, his consciousness-touch extending toward the ancient Giant with warmth that three millennia of shared resistance had forged. "You and I. Igigi and Tidecaller. We prove that cooperation can persist even when everything else fractures. We document what happened here—the ritual, the betrayal, the mechanisms of manipulation. We preserve that knowledge so that someday, somehow, when beings with power we don't possess attempt to heal what the Sundering broke, they'll understand what they're fighting against."

"And if no such beings ever emerge? If the Fifth Coming arrives and finds Giants still fractured, still isolated, still carrying suspicions that feel organic because no one remembers they were planted?"

"Then we'll have failed permanently," Pazuzu admitted. "But the alternative—accepting the fragmentation, allowing the lies to become truth through lack of resistance—that guarantees failure. At least this way, we choose defiance even if defiance proves insufficient."

Torhh rumbled acknowledgment, its damaged stone-flesh settling into the geological patience that had sustained it across centuries of occupation. The Tidecaller looked toward the ocean where Brajor allies had died despite their choice to resist programming, toward the mountains where Ash Giants were accepting Anunnaki healing treatments, providing intelligence on other tribal movements, allowing Needle infrastructure to be maintained through their territories without resistance.
