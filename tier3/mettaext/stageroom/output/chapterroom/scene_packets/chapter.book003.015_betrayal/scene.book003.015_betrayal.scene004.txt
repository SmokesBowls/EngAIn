scene 015.4 — the discovery

scene meta:
location: Tidecaller Sanctuary & Regional Giant Territories
time: year 10,550, month 2, day 14
participants: Torhh, Pazuzu, Saresh, Giant tribal leaders
focus: Torhh and Pazuzu discover the Ash Giants' collaboration via unencrypted Anunnaki Needle broadcasts; the revelation drives the remaining tribes into permanent isolation, fulfilling Queen Eduhauana's multi-millennial strategy to shatter indigenous trust.
continuity: 34 days after the Ash Giant vote in scene 015.3
presentation: cutscene
cutscene purpose: Transmission of the Ash Giant betrayal across the Needle network and the total collapse of inter-tribal trust.

The other tribes learned of the betrayal through Needle surveillance that the Anunnaki ensured wasn't encrypted. The message was blunt: "Ash Giant territories secured through cooperative arrangement. Intelligence sharing commencing. Recommended application: additional pressure on remaining resistant factions."

Torhh received the notification through consciousness-channels that centuries of Needle maintenance had made familiar, and felt something ancient break in its awareness.

"They chose them," the Tidecaller rumbled to Pazuzu, who stood beside the ancient Giant as they had for three thousand years. "After everything. After the Garden. After the First Coming's resistance. After we suffered together in the convergence. They looked at their pain and decided cooperation with the golden ones was preferable to alliance with us."

"We blamed them," Pazuzu replied quietly, his mathematical consciousness processing the implications with brutal clarity. "During the ritual, when the network fractured, we accused them of weakness. Of forcing the convergence through their inability to defend their mountains. We made them feel that requesting our aid would mean confirming our judgment."

"So this is our fault?"

"This is the Queen's design, executed perfectly across three thousand years of patient manipulation. She planted lies that isolated them. She deployed Gruulith specifically to break them. She knew the ritual would fracture alliances along existing suspicions. And she calculated that when we blamed them for requiring rescue, they would hear confirmation that they had always been separate, always been judged, always been alone."

Torhh's ocean-deep eyes carried grief that geological patience couldn't absorb. "The other tribes are already fragmenting further. The Forest Giants withdraw to their territories, certain that any cooperation makes them vulnerable to similar betrayal. The Mountain clans hoard their knowledge more tightly, trusting no one. The Subterranean populations—already isolated—see confirmation that surface alliances are impossible."

"And the Sundering is complete," Pazuzu finished, his voice carrying three millennia of witnessed tragedy. "Not through the lies alone. Not through the ritual's suffering. But through the combination—suspicions planted during the First Coming finding perfect conditions to grow when the Second Coming's pressure made cooperation feel more dangerous than isolation."

"Can it be healed? In time, can we—"

"No," Pazuzu said, certainty that mathematics provided when hope could not. "Because the Ash Giants' choice will be used as justification for mistrust in every future crisis. When the Third Coming arrives, tribes won't attempt unity because they'll remember this moment. When the Fourth brings humans into the conflict, cooperation will seem impossible because any ally might become collaborator. And by the Fifth Coming—three thousand years from now—Giants won't even remember they were once unified. The Sundering will be so complete that your species will think it natural, inevitable, the way things have always been."

In the northern mountains, Ash Giants were accepting Anunnaki healing treatments, providing intelligence on other tribal movements, allowing Needle infrastructure to be maintained through their territories without resistance.

In the southern deserts, Saresh and the survivors were retreating into the deepest chambers, certain that surface cooperation led only to betrayal.

In the forests and highlands and subterranean depths, tribes were calculating isolation as the only viable strategy, each convinced that the Ash Giants' choice proved what three thousand years of subtle programming had suggested: other factions could not be trusted when pressure became sufficient.

The Nephilim had completed their purpose—eliminated Anunnaki creatures, fulfilled cosmic law's requirements for balance. They departed as efficiently as they'd manifested, their consciousness returning to quantum foam, their forms dissolving back into the vrill-energy from which they'd been constructed.

They left behind Giants who had summoned defenders through unified suffering, and emerged too fractured to defend each other.
