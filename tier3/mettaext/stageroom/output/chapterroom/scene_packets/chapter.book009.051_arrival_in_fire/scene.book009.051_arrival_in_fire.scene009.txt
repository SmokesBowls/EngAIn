ACT 5: RETURN TO COIL

They stumbled, half-blind and burned, back to the Spire chamber. Coil's rotation, which had been a frantic wobble, had smoothed to a steady, graceful turn.

His voice, when it came, was the clear harmonic of a functioning machine. "I feel it. The flow is restored. Pressure is stabilizing. The planetary systems are… recalibrating. The fever breaks."

The light in the chamber softened. The hairline fractures in the walls ceased weeping molten light.

"The planet will survive," Coil stated. A pause, a flicker of something deeply personal in his facets. "Thank you. I was… I was never meant to do this alone."

Luminaire's presence warmed in Geralt's mind, weary but satisfied. "The Spire is stable. Returning to managed dormancy. The war above continues, but Pyroclast… Pyroclast will not die today."

Outside, with a gust of wind and the scent of ozone, Aelxion landed. The Warden of Breach looked at them, then at the now-gentle pulse of the Spire.

"The worm's death bought us time," he rumbled. "Not peace. The Anunnaki will dig elsewhere. The dragons will still fight. But time… time is the rarest currency in a war. You have given us a little more."

Geralt turned to Kyh, who was leaning against a wall, shaking from exhaustion. "You're with us now?"

Kyh looked at his hands, still faintly trailing shadow-smoke. He looked back the way they'd come, toward the war he was leaving. "I walked the Spire's paths for months without knowing what they were. I used them to save a handful. Today, I used them to save a world." He met Geralt's gaze, his twilight eyes finding a hard resolve. "Now I know what they are. And I know what they're for. Yes. I'm with you."

ACT 6: DEPARTURE

A soft, silver-gold light coalesced in the center of the chamber. Luminaire's emergency gate, powered by the last of her reserves and the newfound stability of the Pyroclast node.

Kyh hesitated at the threshold, looking back one last time at the burning sky of his home.

Oreck placed a heavy hand on his shoulder. "You're not leaving them to die. You're going to help cut the disease out of the network that's making this war inevitable. That's how you save them all."

Kyh took a deep, shuddering breath, and nodded.

They stepped through the gate.

Pyroclast vanished behind them, still a world at war, still a landscape of fire and ash, but now, beneath the chaos, a living heart beat steadily once more.

Partial victory.
Two spires stabilized.
Three to go.
A new navigator acquired.
The real enemy still hidden in the data-streams, watching, waiting.

The campaign continued.
