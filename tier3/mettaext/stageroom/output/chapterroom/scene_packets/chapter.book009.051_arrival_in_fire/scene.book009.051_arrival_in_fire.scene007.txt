Epic 3: THE WORM

ACT 1: THE MAZE

The caldera wasn't a cave. It was a claustrophobic cathedral of failed geology.

They descended into a maze of collapsed magma arteries, tunnels that had burned hollow, then been crushed by seismic shifts. The air wasn't just hot—it was aggressive, searing the lungs with each breath. The walls glowed a dull, dangerous orange, and the floor was unstable, a crust over flows that hadn't fully cooled.

Kyh led, his hands moving in precise, quiet gestures. Shadows pooled at his command, forming fleeting doorways through walls of semi-molten rock. He wasn't creating paths; he was finding the memory of paths that had once existed, the negative spaces the rock had forgotten to fill.

"Left here," he'd whisper, voice strained. "The shadow is thinner. The way is… almost remembered."

Progress was a brutal, methodical slog.

· When a tunnel ahead pulsed with the promise of a flash-vent, Geralt would raise the Hydros Crystal. A wave of cold, silent absorption would wash forward, quenching the thermal build-up, leaving the passage dark and brittle but safe.
· When the way was blocked by a tumble of sharp, glassy rubble, Oreck would plant the Terros Crystal against it. A deep, subsonic thrum would pass through the stone, not exploding it, but convincing it to rearrange, to settle into a stable, passable slope.

The earth is grateful, Green rumbled, feeling the stone's relief at being guided, not shattered.
The heat is confused, Red crackled softly. It wants to flow, not explode.

Deeper they went. The pressure grew, a physical weight on the chest. The light from the walls grew brighter, harsher.

Kyh’s portals began to flicker. He stumbled, catching himself against a hot wall and hissing in pain.

"Something down here is… wrong," he gasped. "It’s not just heat. It’s a… silence in the shadow. The paths don’t want to form. It’s like something is eating the possibility of them."

He is correct, Violet whispered, her harmonics tinged with unease. This deep, reality is not just physical. It is metabolic. Something is consuming potential itself.
