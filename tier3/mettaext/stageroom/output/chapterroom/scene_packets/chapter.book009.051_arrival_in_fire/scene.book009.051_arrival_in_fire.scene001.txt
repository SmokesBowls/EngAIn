ACT 1: THE BURNING SKY

The emergency Void Jump to Pyroclast wasn't a transition—it was a violent expulsion.

They were spat out mid-air into a world screaming in thermal agony. Twin suns burned through an ash-choked sky the color of a fresh bruise. Below them stretched pure, multi-faction chaos.

The Dragon Wars were not a myth here. They were geology.

· Dragons of every elemental allegiance—Golden Flame, Void-touched, Water-callers, Earth-wardens—clashed in aerial duels that painted the sky with fire and lightning.
· VALDRASIL-backed Anunnaki constructs, massive biomechanical horrors, tore at the planet's crust, digging for something deep below.
· Arcturian energy-weavers and Lyrian blade-dancers held desperate ground positions against waves of Zeta Reticulan tech-corpses.
· Pleiadian skim-ships strafed in precise, lethal patterns, targeting the Anunnaki dig-sites.

And through it all, the planet itself was hemorrhaging. Magma vents exploded randomly across the landscape, vomiting fire and molten rock into the already choked air.

Geralt's armor deployed its wings with a thunderous snap, catching them in a sickening lurch. The heat hit them like a physical wall. The applicator flashed: < Ambient Temperature: 127°C. Atmospheric Toxicity: Extreme. Survival Estimate: 17 minutes without protection. >

Fire is screaming, whispered Red, her presence a pained ember in the thermal chaos. It does not want to burn like this.

The earth is being torn, rumbled Green, a deep ache in the bond. It bleeds.

They descended into the hellscape, the dragon armor's wings shedding molten ash with each beat.

ACT 2: AELXION'S INTERCEPTION

They hadn't fallen a hundred feet before something massive and fast intercepted them.

Aelxion, the Warden of Breach, was a dragon of weathered bronze scales and eyes that held the cold, hard light of broken stars. He moved with terrifying precision, cutting off their descent, his maw opening to reveal a plasma-gland ready to incinerate them.

"Another insertion!" his voice boomed, vibrating through Geralt's bones. "More rats for the burning maze!"

"WE'RE HERE FOR THE SPIRE!" Geralt roared back, the armor amplifying his voice.

"Liar! Everyone wants the spire! For conquest! For fire!"

There was no time. Aelxion drew breath to burn them from the sky.

"NOW, LUMINAIRE!"

A pulse of pure, harmonic Keeper-signature—Luminaire's unique resonance—erupted from Geralt, not as an attack, but as a identifier. A calling card of the network's original architect.

Aelxion choked off his fire. The plasma died in his throat. His star-broken eyes narrowed, scanning Geralt with sudden, intense focus.

"You... you're with the Spire? Not just another scavenger?"

"We're here for Aetheric Coil."

A sound like grinding stone escaped Aelxion—a draconic sigh of profound exhaustion. "The planet is screaming. This isn't conquest anymore. It's collapse. If you're here to help Coil..." He looked at the carnage below, his wings beating to hold them steady in the superheated updrafts. "...you're already too late. But follow. Try anyway."

He banked sharply, leading them through a hail of energy fire and falling debris.

ACT 3: BATTLEFIELD ALLIANCE

Aelxion brought them down behind an Arcturian energy-ward—a shimmering dome of blue light holding against a tide of Anunnaki constructs. Inside, a Lyrian commander with skin like polished oak coordinated her blade-dancers with an Arcturian whose form flickered with contained starlight.

"Commander," Aelxion rumbled, landing with a ground-shaking impact. "These two claim they can help Coil. I'm inclined to let them try."

The Arcturian commander, his voice a chorus of harmonics, didn't turn from the ward. "The magma regulation is failing. Chambers are exploding at random. We've lost three squads to flash-venting. If you have a solution, implement it. We'll hold this line for seven more minutes. Then we retreat or die."

No ceremony. No debate. Survival had pared communication down to bare essentials.

Geralt and Oreck joined the line. Oreck's cleaver met Anunnaki alloy with showers of sparks. Geralt's armor tanked searing energy blasts, the plates glowing cherry-red. They fought alongside aliens, a coordinated pocket of chaos in a sea of madness. It was ugly, efficient, and desperate.

A strange harmony, Violet observed softly in Geralt's mind. Different musics, same song of survival.
