ACT 4: DESCENT PREPARATION

Back in the relative calm of Coil’s chamber, the three crystals now hung from Geralt’s armor harness in a rough triangle: the cold blue of Hydros, the steady brown of Terros, the blazing red of Pyros.

Aetheric Coil rotated above his pool, his facets catching the crystals’ light. “The harmony is… correct. The elemental logic is sound. But the caldera maze is a tomb. Collapsed, unstable, flooded with toxic heat and seismic ghosts. Your Shadow Weaver’s portals will be your only map.”

Kyh, who had been quietly observing, rubbed his hands together—a nervous habit. “I can move through shadow, not solid rock. If the way is fully collapsed…”

“Then we dig,” Oreck said, checking the edge of his cleaver. “Or we blow a path. The crystals can interact with the environment. We’ll make a way.”

“I cannot follow you,” Luminaire said, her voice faint. She had manifested a barely-visible shimmer next to Coil. “The depth, the thermal chaos… it would shred my coherence. I will remain here, maintaining the surface network connection. I will… monitor your vital signs through the Anchor bond. If you die, I will know.”

It was the closest she had come to stating concern.

Aelxion, who had remained at the entrance, spoke up, his star-broken eyes serious. “The worm—the Anunnaki seeder—is ancient. It has been buried in the planet’s deepest heat for centuries. It won’t expect visitors. Use that. It is a creature of consumption, not combat. Surprise is your greatest weapon.”

Geralt looked at his team: Oreck, solid and ready; Kyh, nervous but resolved; the three crystals, heavy with sacrifice; and in his mind, the quiet chorus of the Five Mikas, each attuned to the element they were about to confront.

We are with you, Anchor, Violet whispered for them all. The deep dark awaits.

They turned toward the entrance to the lower vents—a gaping maw of darkness and remembered fire that led into the planet’s scorched arteries. The empty caldera yawned before them, a silent, sprawling scar from a past eruption, its passages a labyrinth of collapsed magma tubes and trembling, half-molten rock.

Without another word, they began their descent into the belly of the burning world.
