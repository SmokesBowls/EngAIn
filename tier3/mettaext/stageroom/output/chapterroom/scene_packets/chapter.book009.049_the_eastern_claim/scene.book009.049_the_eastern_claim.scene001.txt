ACT 1: THE BADLANDS SETTLEMENT - STATIC ON THE NETWORK

Geralt and Zephyr traveled east for three weeks. The land changed. The applicator in Geralt’s head fed him a constant, useless stream of data:
Ambient Temp: 41°C. Wind: 12 kph, NNE. Bio-signatures: Sparse (Lizard, rodent, insect).
The data was noise. The truth was in the silence. The network—the Anunnaki energy grid he was bonded to—felt thin here. Stretched. Like an old scar.

The Five Mikas, however, were loud.
As they approached the canyon, a wind (White) sliced past Geralt’s ear, a precise, warning cut. The red dust at his feet (Red, Green) shifted, forming a brief, shimmering pattern—a crude reptilian eye, then a hatchling, before dissolving. A message.
Danger. Family.
