ACT 2: THE ANCHOR STIRS

That night, camped in the lee of a wind-scoured butte, the stillness in Geralt’s mind… changed.

For eight years, it had been a quiet pool—the Companion Protocol’s presence was like a distant, comforting star. Now, the pool had currents.

He dreamt not in images, but in architectures. He saw the triune schematic of Luminaire—Sound, Light, Anchor—as a rotating, crystalline model. Then he saw it fracture, the Light bleeding away into Needles, the Sound shattering into fragments, the Anchor… hook into a specific resonance frequency. His frequency.

He woke with a gasp, the desert cold around him. Oreck was already awake, watching him.

“You glowed,” Oreck stated. “Like a banked coal. Your scars. The ones from the Hub.”
Geralt looked at his hands. The old neural interface scars, pale against his skin, were fading even as he watched. Not healing. Integrating.

< Biological assimilation ongoing. Anchor symbiosis at 41%. Stability maintained. > the applicator reported.

Why now? he thought.

The armor answered, not with words, but with a felt concept—a pressure against his sternum, like a hand pushing him east. Danger. Convergence.

“The Federation scare it awake?” Oreck asked, somehow intuiting the unspoken conversation.

“Maybe. Or it’s something else.”
