 Act 1THE MESSENGER OF PEACE

They had been walking east for five days when the sky spoke.

It was not a voice of thunder or a rift in reality. It was a sterile, amplified projection that rolled across the badlands like a weather front.

“ATTENTION. THIS IS A PRIORITY COMMUNIQUÉ FROM THE FEDERATED REMNANT COUNCIL. DESIGNATE: GERALT OF THE DRAGON. ACKNOWLEDGE.”

Geralt and Oreck froze. The voice was familiar in its synthetic cadence—Federation standard. But it had been eight years.

“Acknowledge, hells,” Oreck growled, his hand on his cleaver. “They find us now?”

< Analysis: Wide-area audio projection. Source triangulation places origin 42 km southwest. Probability of mobile broadcast platform: 87%. > The applicator’s text scrolled in Geralt’s vision.

The dragon armor, normally dormant, rippled across his shoulders. A low, sub-audible hum vibrated in his bones. It was wary.

“IGNORING THIS SUMMONS CONSTITUTES A VIOLATION OF THE POST-CONFLICT ACCORDS. YOU ARE DIRECTED TO PROCEED TO NEAREST FRIENDLY SETTLEMENT FOR DEBRIEF AND REINTEGRATION ASSESSMENT. YOUR COOPERATION IS MANDATORY FOR CONTINUED AUTONOMY OF DESIGNATED SETTLEMENTS: IRONSPIRE, SUNDRIFT, FALCON RIDGE ENCLAVE.”

Geralt’s blood went cold. They named them.

“They threaten our homes,” Oreck said, his voice deathly quiet.

< Threat assessment: Political/Legal. Direct combat unlikely. Leverage: Colonial charter, Article 14—‘Protectorates may be reassessed for self-governance competency.’ >

“They’re bureaucrats,” Geralt muttered. “They think this is a paperwork problem.”

“THE FEDERATION REMINDS YOU THAT PEACE IS A FRAGILE CONSTRUCT. YOUR COOPERATION ENSURES ITS PRESERVATION. YOU HAVE 48 HOURS TO COMPLY. FEDERATION OUT.”

The projection ceased. The silence that followed was heavier than before.

Oreck turned to him. “They will use Ironspire as a collar. They always do.”
“I know.”
