scene 005.3 — cliff stabilization and fertile basin

scene meta:
location: Coastal cliff face (future site of Ironspire)
time: year 93
participants: Mountain Giants, desert Giants, copper-grey underground Giants, Senareth
focus: Multi-clan collaboration stabilizes an 80ft cliff face; debris accumulation at the base creates an accidentally fertile garden basin anchoring the future site of Ironspire.
continuity: Takes place 46 years after the obelisk project in scene 005.2
presentation: undecided

The cliff-shaping project finally stabilized after nearly a century of stubborn persistence.

The mountain Giants working the crescent's edge had lost count of how many catastrophic collapses they'd survived. Wet sand techniques had failed. Vrill-binding without proper foundation had failed. Aggressive reshaping that ignored the stone's natural grain had failed spectacularly, creating landslides that buried their work under tons of debris.

But slowly, through trial and error measured in decades, they learned what worked.

The key was cooperation between different Giant types. Mountain Giants provided understanding of how stone wanted to stand. Desert Giants contributed knowledge of how sand could be convinced to behave like stone through specific vrill-frequencies. Underground copper-grey Giants, who had emerged from the mainland's deep caverns, taught techniques for stabilizing foundations through consciousness-touch that reached dozens of feet below surface level.

The resulting cliff wasn't the towering wall the mountain Giants had originally envisioned. It rose perhaps eighty feet—fatal for humans who would eventually build there, but modest compared to the Giants' ambitions. Yet it was stable. Permanent. And the debris of a hundred failures had accumulated at its base, compressed over decades into a basin of such rich mineral content that plants grew there with unusual vigor.

"We built a garden by accident," one of the mountain Giants observed, their charcoal-grey form standing at the cliff's edge, looking down at the unintended consequence of their stubbornness. "Wanted height. Got fertility."

Senareth, visiting the site for the first time in years, felt the Prime connection pulse with recognition. The Aeon Keepers could see what the Giants couldn't yet—that this basin, this cliff, this accidentally-fertile ground would someday anchor a city. That humans would build into the wall, create dwellings and fortifications, call it Ironspire and make it central to conflicts that hadn't begun yet.

But that was future-knowledge. For now, it was just a stubborn project finally complete, proving that persistence could overcome even catastrophic failure if given sufficient time.
