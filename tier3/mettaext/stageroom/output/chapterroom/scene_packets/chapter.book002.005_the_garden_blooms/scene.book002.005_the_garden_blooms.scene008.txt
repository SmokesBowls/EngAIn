scene 005.8 — the first coming arrival

scene meta:
location: Central valley / Garden monuments
time: year 500
participants: Senareth, Elyraen, Giant clans, Nephoretti assembly, arriving forces, Aeon Keepers (remote warning)
focus: The 500-year Garden era ends during the Festival of First Contact when the sky turns void-black and Anunnaki geometric vessels touch down to harvest the world.
continuity: Concludes the 500-year Garden era; leads directly into the Anunnaki orbital entry in chapter 006
presentation: cutscene
cutscene purpose: Major era transition and reveal of the First Coming invasion

The celebration continued into evening as it had for three days—Giants and Nephoretti gathered in the largest valley, surrounded by the monuments they had built together, sharing harmonics and consciousness-touch and the simple joy of existence in community.

Seraneth stood among the assembled thousands, feeling the Prime connection pulse with something that might have been pride. The Aeon Keepers could see what had been accomplished, could recognize that their thought-seeds had grown into something worth the sacrifice of entering the Ethereal Realm.

The sun set in colors that still made their newly-formed eyes ache with beauty. Orange and purple and red painting clouds that drifted above pyramids and obelisks, above mountain peaks and forest canopies, above a world that had learned to sing.

Then the colors began to dim.

Slowly at first—so gradually that most didn't notice immediately. But Seraneth, whose consciousness remained perpetually attuned to subtle changes, felt wrongness settle over the valley like cold fog.

The sunset was fading. Not naturally, not with the smooth progression of day yielding to night, but unnaturally. As if something was interposing itself between sun and sky, blocking light that should still be reaching them.

Giants noticed next. Their harmonics shifted from celebration to confusion, then to something approaching alarm. The temperature dropped—not seasonal cooling, but abrupt chill that made even stone-fleshed beings shiver.

Darkness descended at wrong speed, from wrong direction.

Seraneth looked up and felt their entire consciousness recoil from what they saw.

The sky was going black. Not the natural black of night filled with stars, but void-black. Absolute absence of light spreading like infection across the heavens, consuming sunset colors, swallowing clouds, erasing the familiar constellations that had guided them for five centuries.

And in that spreading darkness, shapes descended. Massive beyond comprehension, geometric forms that hurt to perceive directly, surfaces that reflected no light yet somehow remained visible against the void. Vessels. Ships. Constructs that shouldn't exist within physical reality yet were manifesting anyway, forcing themselves into dimensions they didn't belong in.

The celebration died. Music ceased. Harmonics fell silent as thousands of consciousnesses united in collective recognition of what was arriving.

The First Coming.

The Aeon Keepers had known. Had always known. That's why they manifested the Nephoretti when they did, why they gave them five hundred years to build something worth defending. Because this moment was inevitable. The darkness. The vessels. The beings who would descend from them carrying purposes that would shatter the Garden like the Pelagor's paradise had been shattered on Tiamat.

Seraneth felt the Prime connection pulse with grief and determination in equal measure. The Aeon Keepers were sending their final briefing, compressed understanding flooding through dimensions into consciousness barely prepared to receive it:

They come to harvest. To enslave. To reshape this world according to their purposes. You cannot stop them. But you can endure. You can preserve what matters. You can ensure that when they eventually leave, enough remains to rebuild.

The first vessel touched down on the far side of the island—impact creating tremors that shook pyramids and toppled smaller obelisks. More followed. A rain of geometric impossibility falling from manufactured night, bringing with them cold and fear and the end of everything the Garden had become.

Giants and Nephoretti stood together in the valley, surrounded by monuments to their cooperation, and watched their world die in darkness and descending shadow.

The Garden had bloomed for five hundred years.

The harvest was beginning.
