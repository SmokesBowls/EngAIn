scene 010.1 — return of the citadel and sundering evaluation

scene meta:
location: Crimson Citadel, High Orbital Position
time: year 10,500, month 1, day 1
participants: Queen Eduhauana, Tactical Consciousness
focus: Queen Eduhauana returns after three thousand four hundred cycles, reviews sensor data proving the Sundering has degraded Giant tribal cohesion by 58% to 81%, and authorizes the deployment of engineered suppression beasts.
continuity: 10,400 years after the morning after departure in scene 009.4 (and follows the Ironspire epilogue framing in scene 009.5)
presentation: cutscene
cutscene purpose: The Anunnaki fleet returns for the Second Coming; Eduhauana reviews the successful Sundering data and authorizes the deployment of engineered suppression beasts

Queen Eduhauana stood before the observation aperture and calculated satisfaction in mathematics proven correct.

Three thousand, four hundred solar cycles. Longer than most civilizations existed. Longer than the lifespan of stars in certain unstable regions. But for beings who measured time in geological epochs, it was merely an extended pause between strategic moves.

The planet turned beneath her vessel—blue and green and beautiful in its ignorance. The indigenous biologicals who had delayed her First Coming objectives were still there, their populations recovered from the initial occupation's casualties. The Igigi and Reptilian garrison forces she'd left behind had multiplied according to projections, maintaining the partial Needle network through three millennia of autonomous operation.

Everything proceeding as calculated.

But the most satisfying data came from the psychological warfare assessment. Remote sensors embedded in the Needle structures had monitored indigenous consciousness-patterns across the occupation period, tracking the subtle fragmentations she'd engineered before departure.

The Sundering was working.

"Display tribal unity analysis," she commanded.

Probability matrices manifested in the air before her—geometric patterns showing the Giants' social cohesion degrading across millennia. Not catastrophically—that would have been too obvious, potentially triggering defensive unification. But gradually. Persistently. Like erosion transforming landscape through patient application of force too subtle to resist effectively.

Tidecaller/Desert Giant cooperation: 73% degraded from First Coming baseline. Primary tensions: water management, territorial boundaries, ritual scheduling conflicts.

Mountain/Forest Giant alliance: 64% degraded. Disputes over resource allocation, knowledge-sharing protocols, elevated altitude vs. lowland territorial claims.

Highland/Subterranean cooperation: 81% degraded. Almost no interaction across the occupation period. Geographic isolation had amplified programmed suspicions into genuine cultural divergence.

Ash Giant integration: 58% degraded from all other factions. Increasing isolationism, defensive territoriality, resistance to inter-tribal coordination.

"Acceptable," Eduhauana observed, her ancient consciousness processing the implications. The Giants weren't yet at open warfare—that would come later, after proper pressure application. But they were fractured enough that coordinated resistance would prove difficult, that old alliances could be exploited, that desperate factions might accept... alternative arrangements.

"Tactical assessment for complete subjugation," she requested.

The consciousness managing orbital operations responded with calculations that spanned probability space: "Indigenous biological capabilities remain significant despite social fragmentation. Stone-flesh resilience exceeds standard Reptilian combat effectiveness. Consciousness-touch sophistication has evolved over occupation period—Giants demonstrate improved geomantic techniques, possibly through continued Igigi interface. Recommend deployment of purpose-built suppression assets rather than relying solely on garrison forces."

"Approved. Initiate creature deployment protocols. Begin with entry-level assets—Gruulith for mountain regions, Dregans for desert territories, Brajor for coastal zones. Assess indigenous response capability before committing advanced resources."

Eduhauana turned from the observation aperture, her golden skin catching light that made reality itself seem diminished by comparison. Three thousand years of patience were about to yield their calculated return.

The Second Coming had begun.
