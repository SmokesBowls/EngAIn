"Those who choose honor," Elder Pazuzu declared, his ancient form beginning to glow with the accumulated power of eons, "will stand with me. We will face the Anunnaki as our ancestors faced the Void-Spawn. We will remind them that the Igigi do not yield."

"Those who choose survival," Zephyr countered, his mathematical mind already calculating optimal scatter vectors, "will carry forward what we are. What we were. What we might become again. The mathematics of hope require living vessels."

For a moment that stretched across probability space, the choice hung in perfect balance. Honor or survival. Glory or continuity. The fire that burns bright and dies, or the ember that endures through darkness.

Ishara was the first to move, her young form flowing toward the transport pods. "I choose tomorrow," she said simply. "Whatever tomorrow looks like."

One by one, others followed. Not all—Elder Pazuzu and nearly a third of the council remained, their forms beginning to resonate with the harmonics of final battle. But the majority chose Zephyr's path. The path of the scattered seed. The path of mathematical hope.

As the transport pods activated and consciousness-forms dispersed across quantum foam, Zephyr felt the weight of what they had chosen. Not cowardice, as Pazuzu claimed, but perhaps the hardest choice of all—to live with the uncertainty of tomorrow rather than die with the certainty of honor.

The calculation matrix exploded in a cascade of light as Anunnaki breach protocols tore through the chamber's defenses. In the chaos of dimensional displacement, Zephyr caught one final glimpse of Elder Pazuzu and the others, their forms blazing with the fire of final defiance as Anunnaki battle-forms materialized around them.

Then he was elsewhere, scattered across the quantum foam with the others who had chosen tomorrow. In the mathematical space between stars, he felt the echo of their choice rippling forward through time—a choice that would someday birth magic, resistance, and hope on a small blue world circling an unremarkable star.

The first act of freedom had begun.
