They floated in the silent void, the Ghost Spire’s retrieval tether holding them in a bubble of temporary safety. Behind them, Zar’Vokh completed its decoupling. It didn’t vanish. It receded, becoming a faint, dark silhouette against the starfield, a tomb for a jailed god.

Luminaire’s network map updated in their minds. The cancerous, sickly yellow node of Zar’Vokh winked out. The cascade risk percentage ticked downward: 52% -> 44%.

“Spire-9 is offline. The Hybrid Observer is neutralized. The network’s… immune response has been crippled.”

“Success?” Oreck asked, the word heavy.

“A kind,” Geralt said, flexing his hand, feeling the strange deadness in the marble scars. He looked at Kyh, who was curled in on himself, staring at his light-cast shadow. He looked at Oreck, whose eyes held a new, weary depth. He felt the empty channel where ultimate authority had briefly flowed.

They had traded a weapon for a wound.
