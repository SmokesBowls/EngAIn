Far, far away, in a citadel of quiet, calculated power, Queen Enheduanna received an automated ping. A minor alert in a sea of data: SPIRE-9: JURISDICTIONAL AUTHORITY REVOKED VIA FOUNDATIONAL SIGNATURE.

She did not rage. She did not summon armies. She stopped her work, and a slow, genuine smile touched her lips. It was the smile of a grandmaster seeing an unexpected, brilliant move on the board.

“The Architect’s ghost has teeth,” she murmured to the empty air. “And it has taught its little Anchor to bite.” She made a single, subtle adjustment to a long-dormant plan. The game was no longer about containing chaos. It was about hunting a worthy piece.
