ACT 1: EIGHT YEARS OF PEACE

The world healed. Not completely—some scars never fade—but enough.

Eight years.

Eight years of planting and harvest, of mending walls and raising children who had never heard the scream of a plasma turret. Eight years of trade caravans winding between Ironspire and the Sundrift Spire-town, of festivals under the twin suns, of quiet nights where the only watch kept was for wolves, not war-constructs.

Geralt walked the length of the continent in those years, but always returned to one place.

Falcon Ridge.

The valley was green now. The stream ran clear. The hut they’d built together still stood, though he’d added a stone porch, a chimney, shelves for the trinkets people left for her—carved falcons, polished stones, dried flowers. Every week, without fail, he placed fresh blueflowers at the base of the stone marker. The scarf on the banner was little more than threads now, the bloodstain a pale ghost in the weave.

He did not age.

The dragon armor slept against his skin, a dormant potential. The wings, when he summoned them, were strong, responsive—but he rarely needed them. There was no war to fly toward.

Oreck had left after the fifth year, striding east with a nod and a promise to return if ever called. He never did. The veterans had scattered—some to Ironspire’s guard, some to farm, some to wander. Life moved on.

Ironspire was a city now. Walls three times as high, towers, forges that rang from dawn till dusk. Population: over eight hundred. A marketplace, a school, a temple to no god in particular—just a quiet space for remembrance.

Sundrift Spire-town gleamed at the base of the Star Needle, its people a blend of engineers and mystics, drawn by the spire’s soft, steady glow. They traded with Ironspire, shared knowledge, built a future.

And Falcon Ridge… remained empty. A place of pilgrimage for scholars who came to study Mika’s archives, to sit in the silence where the First Tongue had been transcribed. Geralt was its only permanent resident. The keeper of the quiet.

No Anunnaki signals had been detected in eight years. No corruption blooms. No temporal rifts. The network held. The land, though scarred, was clean.

Peace.

It felt like a held breath.
