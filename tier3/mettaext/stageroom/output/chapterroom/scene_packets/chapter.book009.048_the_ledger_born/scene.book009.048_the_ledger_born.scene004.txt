ACT 6: THE REVELATION

Silence.

The five figures turned. For the first time, Geralt saw them clearly.

Red—a woman with hair like living flame, eyes like coals, skin traced with ember-lines.
Blue—calm, fluid, her form subtly shifting like deep water.
Green—solid, grounded, moss and stone woven into his being.
White—ethereal, barely there, a whisper given shape.
Violet—the most defined, the most present. A woman with eyes that held entire constellations.

They approached. Geralt tried to stand, but his legs buckled. Violet caught him, her touch cool and steady.

One by one, they knelt.

Red first, placing a fist over her heart.
Blue, bowing her head.
Green, a hand pressed to the earth.
White, a slight, respectful incline.
Violet last, meeting Geralt’s gaze as she knelt.

“Mika,” Violet said, her voice layered, harmonics of memory and power.

Then, each in turn:

“Mika.”
“Mika.”
“Mika.”
“Mika.”

Geralt’s breath caught. He understood.

These were not the Five he’d rescued from Xal’Zirith. Not anymore. They were what they had become in eight years of transformation—elemental avatars. And they had chosen a name. Not their own. Hers.

ADDENDUM: THE WEIGHT OF A WORD

Geralt stared at them, the echoes of their voices hanging in the air. Five syllables. Five declarations. One name.

Mika.

They had never spoken before. Not in the slave encmp, not in the aftermath of the 250 erased, not in the last moments on heaven before death took them. Their silence had been part of their nature—elemental forces didn't need words. They were.

But they had chosen this word. This name.

Violet’s voice had been the first—layered, harmonic, as if five voices spoke in unison, yet distinctly hers. Then each in turn: Red’s voice like embers settling in a hearth; Blue’s a clear, deep well; Green’s the rumble of stone on stone; White’s the whisper through pines.

They didn’t just say it. They testified.

Geralt’s understanding shifted. It wasn’t that they had learned to speak. It was that they had found something worth breaking silence for.

“You… took her name,” he whispered, the words raw.

“We carry it,” Violet said. But then she paused, as if sensing the weight of what they had just done. Her gaze—those constellation eyes—softened. “We have spoken many words in our becoming. The language of fire, of tides, of roots, of skies. But this word… this name… it is the first we have spoken aloud. To you. To this world.”

Red stepped forward, her ember-eyes fixed on Geralt. “We chose it. Not because we forgot our own. But because we remembered what she was. What she taught by existing.” Her voice crackled, not with heat, with emotion. “She was the first to see us as more than weapons. More than casualties. She was our commander. Our North Star.. and we are and always be your 5 loyal followers, of course we understand who she was"

Green placed his hand flat on the earth. “When we were unmade, she held the line. When we were lost, she sent you to find us. Her will was the anchor.”

Blue’s form shimmered like a calm sea at dawn. “We are five rivers, but we flow to one sea. Her name is the ocean.”

White simply nodded, her form barely substantial. “She is the silence between our words. The breath before speech.”

Violet looked at Geralt, and for a moment, he saw not a cosmic entity, but the young warrior she had been—the one Mika had trained, had trusted, had grieved. “We speak now because we have something to say. We are the Five Mikas. We are her lesson given form: that protection is not control. That power is not domination. That legacy is not a monument—it is a living thing.”

She placed her hand over her heart, where a human heart would beat. “We say her name to remind the world. To remind you. To remind ourselves. We are not just elements. We are her students. And we are here.”

The silence that followed was different from before. It wasn’t empty. It was full of the echo of that name, now woven into the fabric of their being.

Geralt felt the tears then, hot and unashamed. They weren’t just tears of grief, or gratitude. They were tears of recognition. Mika hadn’t just left a book. She hadn’t just left a memory. She had left a vocabulary of hope, spoken in the voices of the elements themselves.

“She would be proud,” he managed, his voice thick.

Red smiled, a small, fierce thing. “We know.”

And in that moment, Geralt understood the true transformation. They hadn’t just gained power. They had gained a voice. And their first word, their chosen word, was the name of the woman who taught them that even the most broken stories could have a beautiful sequel.

ACT 7: THE GRATITUDE

They helped him to his feet. Around them, Ironspire’s people stared, silent, awed.

“You saved me,” Geralt said.

“You saved us first,” Red said, her voice like a crackling hearth. “From erasure. From nothing.”

“We felt your need,” Blue murmured. “Through the network. Through the bond you still carry.”

“We are connected,” Green rumbled. “To this land. To you. To her memory.”

“We do not stay,” White whispered, her words barely audible. “We watch from between.”

Violet placed a hand on Geralt’s chest, over the dormant dragon armor. “You are not alone, Geralt. Even when you feel solitude. We are here. In the fire, the water, the stone, the air, the aether. We are the Five Mikas. And we remember.”

She leaned forward, pressed her forehead to his—a gesture of deep respect, of kinship. “Tell her we said thank you.”

Then, one by one, they faded.

Red into a shower of sparks.
Blue into rising mist.
Green into sinking stone.
White into a sigh of wind.
Violet last, into a shimmer of violet light that hung in the air for a moment before dissolving.

Geralt stood alone in the courtyard, the broken chain-links of Karvex crumbling to dust at his feet.
