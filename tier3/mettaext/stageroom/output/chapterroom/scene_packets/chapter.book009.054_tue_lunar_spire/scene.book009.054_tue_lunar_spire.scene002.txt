ACT 2: THE MAGES' GUILD — ONE BUILDING, TWO REALITIES

At the center of the vast, geometric stone circle stood… nothing.

And everything.

Geralt felt it first—a resonant tug behind his ribs, a sympathetic vibration from the dragon-scale armor. The plates warmed, and a low, harmonic hum filled his skull, a duet between Zaron's ancient consciousness and the latent magic of this place. Where his eyes saw empty crater floor, his perception saw a spiraling tower of woven light and solidified shadow, piercing the star-flecked blackness.

"There," he breathed.

Kyh stood beside him, her head tilted. "I do not see it. But I feel its… seam. A fold in reality where the threads of cause and effect are braided loosely. It is a threshold."

Oreck stared at the same empty space. His predator's eyes, capable of tracking heat and motion across miles, saw only ancient rock and their own footprints. He stepped forward, reached out a massive clawed hand, and swiped it through the air where the tower's entrance should be. His hand met no resistance.

He withdrew it, looking at his empty claws, then at Geralt. There was no anger in his vertical-pupiled gaze. Only a deep, weary understanding.

"This is not my war," he said, the words not a rejection but an acknowledgement. "Not this part of it. My strength cannot open a door I cannot see."

Geralt turned to him. "Oreck—"

"I will not wait idly," the reptilian interrupted, his voice firm. "This is Orion territory. Quiet, but not dead. I will scout the perimeter. Identify outposts. Guard your blind side." He placed a heavy hand on Geralt's armored shoulder. "Protection sometimes means clearing the field so the mage can work. Do what you must learn. I will ensure you have the time to do it."

With a final nod to Kyh, Oreck turned and loped away, his form soon blending into the monochrome landscape, a warrior purposefully stepping out of a story he could not read.

The Guild isn't a place. It's a state of attunement. You don't enter it—you become worthy of it.

Geralt and Kyh stepped forward together. As they crossed an invisible boundary, the Moon vanished.

They stood in an atrium of impossible geometry. A central spiral staircase, seemingly carved from condensed starlight and the memory of obsidian, wound up into a ceiling that displayed not rock, but a swirling nebula. The air thrummed with a low-frequency pulse—the sound of time pooling. Luminescent orbs drifted like slow thoughts. It was silent, yet full of a pressure that spoke of concentrated thought spanning millennia.

This was the Mages' Guild. The same building that existed in a forgotten valley on Earth, accessible only from places of power. Location was irrelevant. Only resonance mattered.

Kyh gasped, her shadow-form trembling. "The weave here… it is not meant for manipulation. It is a library. A living library of outcomes." She was already drawn toward an archway pulsing with deep blues and blacks, the Archive of Unlight. "I must understand…"

She moved away, leaving Geralt alone in the humming stillness.
