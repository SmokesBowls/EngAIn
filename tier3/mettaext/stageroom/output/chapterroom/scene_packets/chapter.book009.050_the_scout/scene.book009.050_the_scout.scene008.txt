Epic 3: THE DEEP VAULTS

ACT 1: THE LOST EXPEDITION

The darkness of the Deep Vaults was not an absence of light—it was a substance. It pressed against Geralt's armor with tangible resistance, cold and heavy as deep ocean water. The only illumination came from Luminaire's flickering form and the faint glow of Geralt's dragon armor, which seemed to struggle against the oppressive gloom, its light dimmed and strained.

They descended through caverns carved from void-glass, their footsteps echoing in ways that suggested spaces far larger than what they could see. Strange, half-seen shapes moved at the edges of their vision—shadow predators that lunged and retreated, testing their defenses. Oreck's cleaver cut through them with decisive sweeps, the crystalline edge leaving trails of dissipating darkness.

Vek'tar led the way, his chroniton-scarred eyes seeing what they could not. "Left here," he'd whisper, pulling them from a path that would have led into a temporal loop. "Don't step there—that's not ground, it's a memory of ground."

His navigation was the only reason they progressed at all. Without him, they would have been lost in moments.

After what felt like hours, they found the remains of the previous expedition.

A chamber opened before them, littered with skeletons still clad in armor of a design Geralt had never seen—sleek, elegant plates etched with luminous patterns now dark. Luminaire's form pulsed with recognition and sorrow.

"My architects," she whispered. "The first team sent to secure the Shard. They never returned."

Oreck knelt beside one skeleton, its bones fused with the void-glass floor. In its hand, it clutched a shard of its own armor, upon which words had been carved in frantic, desperate strokes:

SOVEREIGN LIES. SHARD IS BAIT. VAULT IS PRISON FOR—

The rest was shattered, the bone beneath it crushed by some tremendous force.

"Not killed by traps or guardians," Oreck said, his voice low. "Something in here did this. Something they weren't expecting."

Vek'tar's good eye widened, the white one glowing faintly. "I see echoes. They brought the Shard here to hide it. But something was already waiting. Something that wanted the Light."

Geralt looked at the shattered warning. "Prison for what?"

No one had an answer.
