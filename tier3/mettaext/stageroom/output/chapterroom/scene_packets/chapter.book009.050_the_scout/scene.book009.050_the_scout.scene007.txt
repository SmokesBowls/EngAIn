ACT 7: THE DESCENT BEGINS

The Herald led them from the throne room down a spiraling staircase that seemed to bore into the planet's heart. The air grew colder, thicker, until each breath felt like swallowing liquid shadow.

Luminaire's flickering form hovered near Geralt. "The Deep Vaults were built by my architects as a prison for entities too dangerous to exist. The Shard was placed there not out of fear of the Umbra, but to protect it from being misused. If he obtains it..."

"He'll have the key to corrupting the entire network from within," Geralt finished.

Vek'tar's chroniton-scarred eyes were wide, seeing timelines flicker. "The paths down there... they're not just spatial. They're temporal. Layered. We could walk for days and emerge seconds after we left, or centuries."

They reached the entrance: two massive doors of void-glass, covered in runes that seemed to move when not directly observed. The doors were already slightly ajar. From within, a dry, whispering wind carried the scent of age and despair.

"Here begins your test," the Herald intoned. "Return with the Shard, or do not return at all. The Sovereign does not tolerate failure."

The doors swung open silently. Darkness, deeper than any they'd encountered, waited within.

Geralt looked at his companions: Oreck, solid and unyielding; Vek'tar, fractured but trying to prove his worth; Luminaire, a ghost of herself. He thought of the Sovereign's lie, of the Shard's power, of the network hanging on the brink.

"Stay sharp," he said. "He's setting us up. We're not just fetching a prize. We're walking into a trap."

Oreck hefted his cleaver. "Then let's spring it on our terms."

They stepped into the darkness. The doors sealed behind them with finality.

The fetch quest had begun. But Geralt knew the real mission: secure the Shard, expose the Sovereign's betrayal, and find a way to stabilize the Spire without becoming pawns in a primordial power's game.

And in the darkness ahead, something ancient and hungry stirred.
