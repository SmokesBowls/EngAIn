ACT 2: THE WELCOME PARTY - PROTOCOL OF ABSENCE

Walking between the shadow entities was an exercise in existential discomfort. They had no faces, no discernible features, yet Geralt felt the weight of their attention—not as eyes, but as a subtle cooling of the air around him, a slight increase in resistance, as if reality itself was examining them.

The applicator in his head had given up entirely, displaying only: < ENVIRONMENT: NON-PARSABLE. THREAT ASSESSMENT: INDETERMINATE. >

The dragon armor remained tense, plates shifting minutely, as if trying to align against a threat with no vector.

After what felt like miles of silent marching, the corridor opened into a vast courtyard before the Spire itself. The structure was architecture turned inside out—buttresses that defied gravity, arches that led into solid walls, stairways that spiraled into their own beginnings. It was carved from crystallized shadow, drinking what little luminescence they carried.

At the base of the grand entrance stood a single figure. Tall, robed in darkness that seemed to drift like smoke. Where a face should be was a smooth, featureless plane that reflected nothing.

Its voice was not sound, but vibration—windchimes made of bone, heard in the marrow.

"The Anchor arrives. Luminaire's vessel. You are expected."

"Are you the Keeper?" Geralt asked, his own voice flat in the dead air.

"I am the Herald. The Umbra Sovereign will see you now." It turned, robe flowing like liquid night. "But understand: you are not here to reclaim property. You are here to negotiate your survival. The terms have changed."
