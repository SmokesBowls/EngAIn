scene 006.4 — reptilian warrior maturation

scene meta:
location: Genesis Chamber Complex, Landing Site Gamma
time: year 1, month 7, day 3
participants: Overseer Ninurta, first-generation Reptilian warrior, tactical consciousness
focus: Overseer Ninurta inspects the first mature nine-foot genetically engineered Reptilian warrior, verifies absolute command integration, and deploys five hundred warriors to security patrols.
continuity: 1 year, 3 months, 22 days after the Igigi arrival in scene 006.3
presentation: undecided

Overseer Ninurta—whose specialty was biological engineering—stood before the first mature Reptilian and assessed its design with clinical precision.

The creature stood nine feet tall on reverse-articulated legs, its body covered in scales that shifted from deep green to mottled brown depending on background. Claws capable of rending stone extended from powerful limbs. A ridge of bone crowned its elongated skull, protecting the enlarged cranial cavity that housed sufficient neural tissue for basic problem-solving and command-following.

Most importantly, the genetic modifications that ensured absolute obedience to Anunnaki consciousness-commands were functioning within acceptable parameters.

"Cognitive assessment," Ninurta commanded.

The Reptilian's eyes—vertical slits that could see across multiple wavelengths—focused on him with appropriate deference. Its consciousness was simple compared to Igigi or Anunnaki, but sufficient for its purpose. It could understand orders. It could apply violence when directed. It could recognize indigenous biologicals as threats when programmed to do so.

"Command structure integration," Ninurta continued, extending consciousness-touch into the Reptilian's neural pathways. The creature's mind opened without resistance, its genetically-mandated obedience making it the perfect tool. "Acknowledged. Overseer authority recognized. Awaiting directives."

Perfect.

Ninurta moved through the genesis chamber, inspecting the hundreds of other Reptilians in various stages of maturation. The first generation was nearly complete—five hundred warriors ready for deployment. The second generation was already developing in their crystalline wombs, genetic templates refined based on performance data from the first batch.

By the end of Year Two, they would have three thousand Reptilians. By Year Five, ten thousand. Sufficient for any containment operations the indigenous biologicals might require.

"Deploy first generation to security positions," Ninurta instructed the chamber's tactical consciousness. "Assign patrol routes around manufacturing complex. Interface with Igigi workforce coordinators. Prepare combat protocols should indigenous biological interference occur."

The Reptilians moved with impressive coordination despite their youth, their genetically-enhanced instincts allowing them to organize into units without requiring extensive training. They were not intelligent in the way Igigi were intelligent—they couldn't calculate probability matrices or craft sophisticated consciousness-touch techniques. But they could follow orders. They could apply violence efficiently. They could serve as the iron fist should the Anunnaki's careful planning require forceful correction.

Ninurta watched them march from the genesis chambers toward their assigned positions and felt satisfaction in work well executed. The biological engineering was sound. The genetic modifications were stable. The creatures were tools, and tools should perform their function without complaint or hesitation.

He did not think about the Earth reptiles whose genetic material had been harvested for this project. Did not consider that these Reptilians were as much victims of Anunnaki manipulation as the Igigi who served as slave labor. Why would he? They were constructs, purpose-built for specific functions, their consciousness intentionally limited to prevent the troublesome self-awareness that made other species difficult to manage.

They were perfect servants. And perfection required no moral consideration.
