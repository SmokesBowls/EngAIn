cutscene purpose: Historical epilogue framing the legacy of the First Coming, the preservation of truth in Ironspire, and future Comings

In the deep places beneath Ironspire, where Nephoretti scholars preserved what the Anunnaki tried to erase, ancient records told a story most had forgotten:

Once, Giants were unified. Once, cooperation between tribes was natural rather than negotiated. Once, before the Sundering, they built wonders that made even Anunnaki infrastructure seem crude.

That unity was broken not by conquest but by whispers. By lies so subtle they became truth across generations. By divisions manufactured to serve purposes the victims never recognized until too late.

But in those same deep places, other records existed. Igigi chronicles documenting three thousand years of choosing alliance despite programming. Giant oral histories preserving memories of how friendship was built from wreckage. Nephoretti observations of how the Sundering was resisted even when resistance seemed impossible.

The Second Coming would test whether that resistance proved sufficient.

The Nephilim would be summoned through desperation and barely-sustained cooperation.

The alliance would fracture immediately after, some Giants choosing Anunnaki promises over unified resistance.

And three thousand years beyond that, in the Fifth Coming that approached like inevitable mathematics, heroes would attempt to heal what the First Coming had broken—to reunite tribes whose division had become cultural identity, to rebuild trust that manufactured lies had shattered, to somehow forge unity from fragmentation that had calcified across geological time.

Whether they would succeed remained unwritten.

But the fact that they would try—that Igigi and Giants had spent three millennia proving consciousness could resist programming through persistent choice—meant the attempt was possible.

And possibility, however slim, was more than the Anunnaki's perfect mathematics had calculated.
