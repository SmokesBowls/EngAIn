{type:narration} THE TRANSFORMATION
{type:blank}
{type:narration} What followed was beyond description in any language Zephyr's mathematical consciousness could access.
{type:narration} Water—vast, impossible quantities of water—roared down from the mountains as millennia of accumulated ice remembered its liquid nature all at once.
{type:narration} The sound was like the planet screaming, like the voice of every storm that had ever existed calling out in harmony, like the Deep Tongue spoken with such power that reality itself bent to accommodate the meaning.
{type:narration} But threaded through that apocalyptic symphony, Zephyr could hear something else: Torrhen's consciousness expanding, dissolving, becoming something unprecedented in the history of existence itself.
{type:narration} The Giant's individual identity was scattering like seeds on storm wind, each fragment carrying the complete synthesis of mathematical magic and mystical intuition they had developed together.
{type:narration} His awareness was merging with the water itself, with the stone it carved, with the lightning that danced between raindrops, with the very quantum foam that underlay physical reality.
{type:narration} Zephyr's magical-mathematical protocols kept him alive through the initial hours of transformation—consciousness-extension techniques allowing him to flow with the flood rather than being destroyed by it, harmonic frequencies creating temporary shelters in the spaces between water molecules, probability calculations guiding him toward the increasingly rare pockets of survivable space.
{type:narration} But survival was not mere existence.
{type:narration} As the world remade itself around him, as continents sank and new ones rose, as the entire biosphere reorganized according to patterns that existed beyond geological precedent, Zephyr felt his own consciousness changing in response to the loss of the only friend he had ever known.
{type:narration} The mathematical precision that had defined his existence for centuries began expanding, incorporating variables that couldn't be quantified: grief, love, the weight of promises made to the dying, the responsibility of carrying forward knowledge that no single consciousness should have to bear alone.
{type:narration} The mystical intuition that Torrhen had taught him began deepening, no longer requiring conscious effort to access the harmonic frequencies that connected all existence.
{type:narration} The Deep Tongue flowed from him now like breathing, like heartbeat, like the natural expression of consciousness that had learned to recognize itself in forces larger than individual identity.
{type:narration} He was becoming something new.
{type:narration} Something that had never existed.
{type:narration} He was becoming the Sage.
