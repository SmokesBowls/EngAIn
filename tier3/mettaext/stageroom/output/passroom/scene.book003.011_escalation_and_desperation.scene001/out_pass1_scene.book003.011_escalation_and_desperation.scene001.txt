{type:narration} The physiological templates were flawless, reverse-engineered from data collected across a dozen purged worlds.
{type:narration} The failure was metaphysical.
{type:narration} The consciousness that awoke within the Dregan form was Dragon-adjacent—proud, ancient, and vast—but it was trapped in a physical shell that was a mockery of that heritage.
{type:narration} They possessed the soul of a storm, caged in a body of earth and stone.
{type:narration} Wings, beautiful and intricate, were grafted to frames too dense and heavy for flight, leaving them eternally earthbound.
{type:narration} They were creatures of air and fire condemned to crawl.
