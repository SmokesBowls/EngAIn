{type:narration} Torrhen's physical recovery had been remarkable, his bio-form's adaptive capabilities enhanced by magical techniques that operated on principles the Sage was still learning to understand.
{type:narration} But more than healing, their first extended exchanges had revealed depths of knowledge and wisdom that complemented his own understanding in ways that felt like recognition rather than discovery.
{type:dialogue, speaker:Torrhen} "The Deep Waters remember everything,"

{type:dialogue, speaker:they} "Prison,"

{type:narration} But this Torrhen brought perspectives his first teacher had never possessed.
{type:narration} Where his friend had been intuitive, mystical, operating through harmony with natural forces, this being combined intuitive wisdom with geological experience that spanned time periods the Sage could barely calculate.
{type:dialogue, speaker:he} "How long?"

{type:narration} "Since the Culling.
{type:narration} Since the sky-devourers came with their mathematics of destruction and their calculated approaches to consciousness-harvesting.

{type:narration} His consciousness recoiled from the implications.
{type:dialogue, speaker:he} "Three millennia of isolation,"
{type:dialogue, speaker:Torrhen} "Not entirely alone,"
{type:narration} The weight of that loneliness settled between them like a physical presence.
{type:narration} The Sage felt his own fifteen years of solitude—already a burden that had seemed beyond endurance—dwarfed by the magnitude of isolation this being had survived.
{type:dialogue, speaker:he} "How?"
{type:dialogue, speaker:Torrhen} "Purpose,"

{type:narration} The probability matrices he had been calculating for decades, the convergences approaching Year 3,500, the necessity of preparing consciousness-crafters for challenges that would make their individual survival struggles seem trivial—Torrhen had reached the same conclusions through completely different means.

{type:narration} Where Torrhen's methods were intuitive but sometimes unfocused, the Sage's mathematical precision provided structure and efficiency.
{type:narration} Where the Sage's approaches were logical but sometimes rigid, Torrhen's mystical flexibility opened new possibilities for creative application.

{type:narration} Not the careful cooperation of allies with shared goals, not the formal respect of teacher and student, but the deep connection that forms between consciousness-forms that recognize something essential in each other.
