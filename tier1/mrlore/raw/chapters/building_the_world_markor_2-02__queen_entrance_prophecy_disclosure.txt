SCENE 2.2: "Queen's Entrance & Prophecy Disclosure"

The temperature shifted first—not cooling, but a subtle change in the way heat moved through the crystalline space, as though the air itself had learned new patterns of respect. Rongtai's shadows stirred without her command, drawn toward a presence that commanded reverence from forces beyond conscious will.

Queen Lyralei Moonheart materialized from the space between heartbeats, her aurora robes rippling with colors that reflected not just light but emotional resonance. At three hundred and seventeen years, she moved with the fluid grace of someone who had learned to carry cosmic responsibility without letting it bend her spine. But Thang caught the nearly imperceptible hesitation in her step, the way her luminous eyes lingered too long on the displays showing Nibiru's approach.

Her robes flickered—just once—from serene blues to anxious gold before settling back into controlled radiance.

"So it returns," she said softly, her voice carrying undertones that seemed to resonate in frequencies beyond normal hearing. Each word fell with the measured cadence of one who had spent centuries learning to shape reality through careful speech. "The cycles turn, as they always have. As they always must."

But her hands betrayed her, moving in small ritual patterns against her robes—ancient gestures meant to ward off prophecies that were already manifesting.

"Your Majesty." Rongtai and Thang approached the lunar throne, its crystalline structure pulsing with the same rhythms that governed tidal flows and dream cycles. The formality felt strange now, weighted with implications none of them wanted to voice.

"The old prophecies speak of this time," she continued, her voice taking on the ceremonial tones that marked the recitation of sacred knowledge. "The Fifth Coming. When the Wanderer returns from the deep void. When the ancient enemies wake from their patient slumber. When..."

Her voice caught. Actually caught, like a song encountering a broken string.

"When the throne will pass to one who carries both lunar shadow and terrestrial earth magic."

The words hung between them like a bridge that none of them wanted to cross.

"That's impossible," Thang said flatly, military precision masking the emotion that threatened to crack his voice. "The lunar throne has been occupied for three millennia. You've guided us through crises that would have—"

"Broken lesser rulers," the Queen agreed gently, moving to stand beside the great window. "But not this one, Thang. This crisis was designed specifically to break us. To break me."

"You don't understand. The throne was never meant to remain here forever. We have been..." She searched for words. "Caretakers. Guardians. Holding the power in trust until the one could emerge who would need it most."

"The heir who is awakening on Earth—she carries something neither of our peoples have possessed alone. Earth magic and lunar magic, unified in a single consciousness. The bridge between worlds that our civilization has been preparing for three thousand years."
