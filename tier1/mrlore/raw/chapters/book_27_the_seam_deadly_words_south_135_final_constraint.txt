The alignment would come when it came, and not before, and the network's task was not to summon it but to survive until it arrived.

The year was approaching its midpoint. The sky was whitening by fractions. The porcelain end was still distant, still advancing, still indifferent. The occupation watched without seeing. The lightning struck on schedule. The warmth endured. The center held.

And somewhere in the deep stone, beneath the grove and the corridor and the occupied city, the thing that had been waiting since before the thread was severed began, very slowly, to stir. Not in response to the forced lock. Not in reaction to the near-catastrophe. In response to something simpler: the network had learned its limit. The network had stopped trying to force what it could not hold. The network had become ready—not ready to complete the name, but ready to receive the conditions under which the name could complete itself.

The stirring was small. A shift of pressure. A deepening of warmth. A new frequency, not yet audible, not yet measurable, that did not split into two tones.

The center had been listening. The center had heard the network's final lesson. And the center, which had pressed grooves into stone and waited for millennia, had decided that the waiting was almost over.
