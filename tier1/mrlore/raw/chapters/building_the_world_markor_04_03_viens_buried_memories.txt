SCENE 04.03: "Viên's Buried Memories"
[Character Backstory/Trauma Revelation Scene]

Viên remained close to his newfound brother, though the events of the previous night had left him struggling to understand transformations that seemed to be occurring on multiple levels simultaneously. The healing he had performed still mystified him—where had that power come from? Why had his blood responded to injury and suffering with such certainty and effectiveness? What were the deeper implications of abilities that seemed to draw upon sources of knowledge and energy that existed far beyond his previous understanding of what was possible?

[EXPANSION: Viên's Trauma Response]

But beneath his confusion and wonder, something else stirred in the depths of his consciousness. When the conversation had turned to chains and captivity, when the others had spoken of Ironspire's facilities for studying and controlling magical abilities, he had felt an instinctive revulsion that surprised him with its intensity.

The word facilities triggered something deeper than conscious memory.

[SENSORY FLASHBACK]
Cold metal against his cheek. Blue energy restraints humming like angry wasps. A voice speaking Igigi: "Subject Seven resisting neural calibration." The smell of ozone and voided bowels. A needle's bite in his neck—

Viên collapsed, blood streaming from his nose onto thawing earth. The serpent mark beneath his skin pulsed like a fresh burn, sending waves of agony through his nervous system. His hands shook too violently to heal even a scraped knee.

When Garric hauled him up, Viên's vision swam with afterimages: smooth black tables in research wings, beings in white coats with no eyes, restraints that responded to thought rather than movement. Ironspire's research facilities used the same design principles he'd seen in... where? When?

[END EXPANSION]

His blood carried memories that his conscious mind couldn't access, experiences of struggles against forces that sought to reduce consciousness to servitude, knowledge of battles fought across multiple dimensions in defense of the right of all beings to determine their own destiny. The mark that had appeared beneath his skin during the awakening ceremony pulsed with energies that connected him to healing traditions that reached back to the dawn of awareness itself, but also to responsibilities that would require him to become something far more than a simple village healer.
