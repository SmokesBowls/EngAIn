SCENE 4.10: "Ulric's Recognition"**
**[Professional Respect/Mission Adaptation Scene]**

But Ulric was not simply a military commander—he was an intelligence professional whose survival had depended on his ability to read situations accurately and respond to threats that might not be immediately obvious. As the apparent cooperation of the Sundrift survivors continued, he began to sense that something was not quite right about the situation he was managing.

When his awareness finally focused on the absence of the two individuals whose capture had been the primary objective of his mission, his recognition of what had occurred was accompanied by a mixture of professional respect and personal frustration. He had been outmaneuvered by people he had underestimated, manipulated by individuals whose capabilities extended far beyond what his intelligence sources had suggested.

His immediate instinct was to order pursuit, to deploy his forces in an effort to track down and capture the escapees before they could disappear entirely into terrain that would make conventional search and seizure operations extremely difficult. But his professional assessment of the situation made clear that such efforts would likely prove futile while potentially compromising his ability to complete the secondary aspects of his mission.

"Stand down," he ordered, his voice carrying the authority of command tempered by pragmatic recognition of changed circumstances. "Secure the perimeter and tend to the wounded. But for now, we fulfill our original mission. These people need help, and they'll get it."

His decision reflected both professional competence and personal integrity. Whatever his opinions about the broader implications of magical awakening and its potential threats to established authority, he was not willing to abandon humanitarian responsibilities in pursuit of objectives that had become unattainable through changed circumstances.

"The offer of protection stands," he announced, his voice carrying to all of the survivors who had chosen to accept his assistance. "But I want to be clear about something. I know what I saw. I know there are others among you with... capabilities. If you choose to stay, that's your decision. But if you choose to follow your friends... Remember that Ironspire has a long reach and longer memory."
