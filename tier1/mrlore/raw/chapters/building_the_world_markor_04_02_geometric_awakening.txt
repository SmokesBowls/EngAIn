SCENE 4.2: "The Geometric Awakening"
[Power Discovery/Environmental Mystery Scene]

Near the edge of the camp, where the ancient stone formations provided natural shelter from both wind and observation, Tran stood in quiet contemplation of changes that went far beyond the visible marks of his transformation. The crimson pulse in his blood had settled into a steady rhythm, no longer the wild surge of power from the night before, but something deeper, more controlled, more fundamentally integrated with his essential nature.

[EXPANSION: Tran's Channel Resistance]

The geometric channels called like a lodestone in his blood, but Tran deliberately stepped off the carved path to test his own agency. The earth trembled beneath his boots. His palms blistered as crimson energy forced him back onto the precise line carved into the hillside.

"It's not choosing," he rasped to Viên, showing his burned hands. "It's corralling."

The wet stone and ozone stench stung his nostrils. Where his boot had touched the channel's edge, glowing fractals bloomed like luminous lichen, spreading in mathematical patterns that hurt to look at directly. A subsonic hum vibrated in his molars—too regular to be natural, too precise to be accidental.

Not irrigation channels. The realization hit him like a physical blow. Stargate calibration grids. Earth's hidden infrastructure, waiting for activation. The geometric patterns weren't ancient decorations—they were part of a vast navigation system designed to guide cosmic forces through dimensional space.

[END EXPANSION]

He could feel it waiting beneath his conscious awareness—not as something foreign that had been imposed upon him, but as something that had always been part of him, sleeping until the right circumstances arose to allow its awakening. The scythe that rested in his hands no longer felt like a simple farming tool repurposed for emergency defense. It had become something else entirely, a weapon forged in cosmic fires and tempered by will that connected him to legends and destinies whose scope he was only beginning to grasp.

But perhaps more significantly, he could feel the geometric water channels that crossed the landscape like veins carrying the lifeblood of some vast, slumbering consciousness. The precise lines carved into the hillside by ancient hands seemed to pulse with subtle energy that responded to his presence in ways that made the hair on his arms stand up and sent shivers of recognition through his awakened blood.
