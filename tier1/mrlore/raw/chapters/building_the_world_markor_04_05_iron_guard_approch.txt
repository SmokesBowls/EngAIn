SCENE 04.04: "Iron Guard Approach"
[Military Threat/Surveillance Revelation Scene]

At the camp's perimeter, where responsibility for security had fallen to those whose awakened abilities made them most capable of detecting and responding to threats, Thang and Rongtai maintained their careful disguise as refugees who had happened to possess unusual capabilities rather than cosmic entities whose true nature would reshape everything the others understood about their situation.

[EXPANSION: Thang/Rongtai Disguise Strain]

Rongtai's fingers bled where she'd clenched them to suppress her cosmic aura. Every instinct screamed at her to shadow-step away from the approaching military force, but revealing their true nature now would doom any chance of finding the heir they'd been sent to locate and train.

Thang's golden eyes had dimmed to brown through sheer force of will, but the effort left him trembling. Phoenix fire wanted to blaze, especially in the presence of forces that threatened those he'd sworn to protect. Sweat beaded on his forehead from the strain of containing solar radiance that demanded expression.

[END EXPANSION]

"So what do you think?" Thang asked quietly, his voice pitched to carry only to his companion's enhanced hearing. "These are just farmers. I can't see nobility amongst the girls, you?"

Rongtai's response carried the weight of centuries spent searching for individuals whose bloodlines carried the potential for cosmic awakening. "It's hard to say," she replied, her dark eyes studying Keen with the intensity of one who had learned to read possibilities that existed beyond surface appearances. "Is she meant to look regal before she takes the crown? Let's just hide in the safety of numbers as long as we can. See where this group takes us."

[EXPANSION: Military Approach Details]

The sound started as a vibration in the soles of their feet—rhythmic, mechanical, distinctly non-natural. Joriic Longbow, whose hunting experience had taught him to distinguish between different types of movement across terrain, raised his head like a wolf scenting danger.

"Twenty riders," he announced grimly. "Full military kit. Moving in formation along the river road."

The approach wasn't rushed or chaotic like raiders seeking quick plunder. This was methodical, professional, organized—the kind of movement that spoke of specific objectives and predetermined plans. Dust clouds rose in perfect intervals, marking a column that moved with the disciplined precision of forces accustomed to coordinated operations.

[END EXPANSION]
