Here's the condensed version I'd save:

PGT SCALE & REASONING BASELINE

CORE CONVERSION

- 1 PGT Unit = 10 Pixels
- PGT Units are reasoning units, not sprite pixels.
- Pixels are visual representation only.

HUMANBASE

- Human Standard Envelope = HWD(10,4,3)
- Human Slim Envelope = HWD(9,3,2)
- Human Standard Pixel Envelope = 100x40x30
- Minimum Character Sprite Height = 96px

AUTHORITY RULE

- PGT owns spatial reasoning and occupancy requirements.
- Trixel/Godot own visual representation.
- Visual meshes may be smaller than the reasoning envelope.
- Reasoning envelope must always be equal to or larger than the visual body.

STRUCTURE SCALE

- Humanbase Height = 10 PGT = 100px
- Minimum Walkable Structure = 15 PGT = 150px
- Standard House = 20 PGT = 200px
- Large Hall / Manor = 30 PGT = 300px
- Cathedral / Temple = 40 PGT = 400px
- Monumental / Tower = 60+ PGT = 600+px

NARRATIVE SCALE CLASSES

- 10 = Human
- 15 = Shack / Minimum Interior
- 20 = Normal House
- 30 = Hall / Authority Space
- 40 = Cathedral / Sacred Space
- 60+ = Monumental

GIANTS

- Small Giant = 28 PGT
- Standard Giant = 32 PGT

PGT REASONING PURPOSE

The Prose Geometrical Topologist reasons about:

- Fit
- Clearance
- Visibility
- Reachability
- Dominance
- Occupancy
- Access
- Pathing
- Relative Scale

The PGT does NOT reason about:

- Pixel dimensions
- Mesh shape
- Texture size
- Rendering details

The Topology Artifact stores spatial truth. Rendering systems convert that truth into sprites, trixels, meshes, and engine transforms.This captures the conversion system, the scale ladder, and the authority boundary without all the research discussion.
