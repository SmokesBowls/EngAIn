### Critical Design Principles

- **World cell never depends on art.** Mechanics survive skin swaps. `skin_id` is a pointer, not a dependency.
- **Blind fills are campaign-breaking.** Fill protection in the adapter is non-negotiable. `force=True` is the only override.
- **Empire and EngAIn are independent.** Empire is autonomous AI orchestration (writes books, coordinates agents). EngAIn is the game engine. ZW protocol connects them but neither depends on the other. Empire is now legacy from the engine's perspective.
- **Defaults describe terrain. Interesting stuff lives in overrides.** A forest cell is blocked. A secret path is an override that makes specific forest cells walkable.
- **Pattern-seeded memory** — TraeAgent starts sessions at higher confidence by inheriting proven artistic patterns.
