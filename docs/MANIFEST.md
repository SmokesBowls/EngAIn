## Archive Rule

Files in `_archive/` are historical receipts, duplicates, older drafts, freeze transcripts, or evidence packs.

They are not active authority unless a human deliberately promotes their contents into one of the active files listed above.

## Conflict Rule

If two files disagree:

1. Active canonical contract wins.
2. More specific subsystem contract wins inside its own domain.
3. Archive files never override active files.
4. Transcript wrapper text is never authority.
