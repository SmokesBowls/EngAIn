## 7. Mechanimation’s Three Laws

### First Law: No Stretch. No Fake Timeline.
- No shared ghost pose.  
- No solver without frame truth.  
- No sprite stretch where a limb should rotate.  
- Every transformation must be accountable to a real cutout body.

### Second Law: The Empty Frame is Allowed.
- Every frame must be allowed to be **empty** until authored, imported, generated, or baked.  
- Emptiness is not failure. Emptiness is the absence of a lie.  
- A frame that is empty but marked empty is honest.  
- A frame that is filled by cheating the timeline is a broken witness.

### Third Law: Autonomy Learns the Middle.
- Autonomy does not skip the middle.  
- Autonomy learns the middle.  
- The middle is where weight lives.  
- The middle is where lies show.  
- The middle is where a walk becomes a walk instead of a flipbook twitch.

## 8. Pass / Fail Conditions

**Mechanimation passes when:**
- Every pose in the sequence exists as an authored or baked entity.
- Every frame has its own metadata (rect, bbox, joint coordinates, authored flag).
- The frame package keeps every slot separate (no cross-frame contamination).
- Interpolation produces a **frame 2** that is meaningfully different from frame 1 and 3.
- The foot arc, torso dip, hip settle, wrist drag, toe roll, and shoulder recoil are either authored or generated with traceable parameters.
- The export metadata proves the position of every joint for every frame.
- Trixel receives a real mask and not a wish.

**Mechanimation fails when:**
- Frame 2 is skipped or mathematically averaged into nothing.
- A sprite stretches to fake rotation instead of rotating the limb.
- A solver produces motion without storing the resulting frame truth.
- The package pretends one pose is twelve frames.
- Metadata is missing or incomplete.
- A generated motion cannot explain itself.

## 9. Hard Reject Conditions (Gate Rules)

EngAInOS / the system MUST reject Mechanimation’s validation if:
- Any frame in the exported sheet lacks an authored flag or joint coordinate set.
- The interpolation path produces a frame that violates bone length constraints.
- Boomerang math is applied to the entire root translation (the walk must progress).
- The frame package merges layers in a way that loses the original limb isolation.
- The solver claims autonomy but cannot show the middle frame.
