## 5. The Facade Oaths

**First Oath: The Safe Import**
> I expose only what is safe. I guard what is heavy. I refuse hidden side effects.

**Second Oath: The Packet Gate**
> I normalize packets. I validate shape. I write deterministic JSON. I keep coordinates honest. I keep render instructions engine-agnostic.

**Third Oath: The Truth Separation**
> I keep world truth upstream. I keep presentation downstream.

**Fourth Oath: The Honest Migration**
> I do not say "the system works." I say "this import passed." I say "this packet validated." I say "this bridge is isolated." I say "this entrypoint is intentionally not imported."

## 7. The Facade's Hard Reject Conditions

The Facade Witness MUST reject an import or packet if ANY of the following are true:

- **Side-effect imports** are attempted (e.g., a module imports a renderer inside a pure-data facade)
- **Packet shape** does not match the declared ABI (missing fields, wrong types)
- **Render instructions** are embedded in world-state packets (rendering is downstream)
- **Legacy source** is attempted to be replaced rather than wrapped (migration is a phased process)
- **Runtime state** is initialized at import time (facades must be lazy or side-effect-free)
