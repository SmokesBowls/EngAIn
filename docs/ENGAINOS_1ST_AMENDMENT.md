# EngAInOS 1st Amendment

This document defines the core lane law for EngAInOS and related systems.

## Prime Rule

No lane may steal another lane's authority.

## Core Definitions

Tier means authority rank.

Lane means work boundary.

Stack means implementation family.

Management means staying inside the lane.

Execution means performing work inside the lane.

Authority means owning truth inside a declared domain.

Render means presentation or composition only. Render is never truth.

## First-Class Authority Lanes

Some lanes are first-class authority lanes inside their own domains.

MrLore owns canon and lore truth.

EngAInOS owns governance, declared truth, contract gates, validation, orchestration, and acceptance into declared state.

Trixel owns asset truth, including asset IDs, atlases, skins, meshes, recipes, manifests, provenance, and asset supersession.

## Execution Lanes

Execution lanes perform work but may not write declared truth by themselves.

GodotSim owns simulation execution.

Engionality owns synchronization execution.

Mechanimation owns motion and action execution.

Terrain owns world-surface and terrain-management execution.

Execution lanes may produce outputs, events, packets, or snapshots.

Execution lane output becomes declared truth only after EngAInOS acceptance.

## Extraction and Translation Lanes

mettaext owns prose-to-structure extraction.

mettaext may produce candidate scene structures, entities, events, and semantic artifacts.

mettaext output is not declared truth until accepted by EngAInOS.

## Asset Lane

Trixel is a peer authority lane, not a Godot dependency.

Trixel may produce 2D, 3D, 4D, or any-D assets.

EngAInOS does not own asset production.

EngAInOS should not care how an asset was produced.

EngAInOS should only care about valid asset references.

## Render and Presentation Lanes

Render layers are management only.

Render layers do not own truth.

Render layers do not create class authority.

Render layers do not declare entities.

Render layers do not declare mechanics.

Render layers only organize how accepted inputs are displayed.

Godot owns presentation only.

Godot may display what it is handed.

Godot may not decide what is real.

## Lane Theft Rules

No render layer may become truth.

No execution lane may commit declared truth.

No asset lane may write mechanics truth.

No governance lane may pretend to own assets.

No simulation lane may promote inferred entities into declared truth.

No presentation client may declare canon.

No lane may overwrite another lane's manifest, contract, or accepted domain truth.

## Acceptance Rule

Only EngAInOS acceptance creates declared project/runtime truth.

GodotSim, Engionality, Mechanimation, Terrain, mettaext, Trixel, Blender MCP, and Godot may all produce useful outputs.

Those outputs remain lane-owned outputs until EngAInOS accepts them into declared truth.

## Boundary Summary

MrLore owns canon truth.

EngAInOS owns declared truth and governance.

Trixel owns asset truth.

GodotSim owns simulation execution.

Engionality owns synchronization execution.

Mechanimation owns motion execution.

Terrain owns world-surface management.

mettaext owns extraction.

Blender MCP owns external 3D tool bridge operations.

Godot owns presentation.

No lane may steal another lane's authority.
