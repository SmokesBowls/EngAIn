## Expanded Doctrine



**Evolving Narrative Gnosis and Individual Nomantics**



## Core Definition



Nomantics means **the law of meaning**.



In EngAIn, a name is not just a label. A name carries meaning, authority, scope, and limits.



A term must not silently drift into powers it was never granted.



## Doctrine



EngAIn is an evolving narrative-knowledge system where named meanings are governed instead of allowed to drift.



Prose owns meaning.



The backend owns safe ingestion, validation, schemas, gates, and mechanical enforcement.



AI agents may prepare, extract, organize, classify, validate, and report.



AI agents may not silently mutate truth, canon, runtime, Godot, ZONJ, passroom authority, or creative meaning.



## Nomantic Law



A term must declare:



- what it means

- who owns its meaning

- what authority it carries

- what it is allowed to affect

- what it is forbidden to imply



## Examples



`PROPOSED` means a claim has been extracted for review.



`PROPOSED` does not mean accepted.



`CONFIRM` means a review decision may support later promotion.



`CONFIRM` does not mean canonized.



`P9_ENTITY_QUALITY_FLAGGED` means a term is quality-flagged for review.



`P9_ENTITY_QUALITY_FLAGGED` does not mean rejected.



`accepted_lore_packet.schema.json` defines the shape of future accepted lore packets.



A schema is not a real accepted lore packet.



Accepted lore is not canon writing.



Canon writing is not runtime mutation.



Godot consumes lawful outputs.



Godot does not decide lore truth.



MrLore prepares lore authority.



MrLore does not own art authority.



Retrographer prepares visual surface authority.



Retrographer does not own lore truth.



EngAIn holds orchestration authority.
