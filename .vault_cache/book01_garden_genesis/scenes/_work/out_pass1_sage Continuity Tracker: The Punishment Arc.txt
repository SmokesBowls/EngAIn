{type:narration} 1.
{type:narration} Event Name: The Choice & The Scatter
{type:narration} * World Era / Calendar System: Year 14000, the third coming.
{type:narration} * Cause: The Anunnaki's oppressive rule and genetic harvesting of the Igigi, coupled with the mathematical certainty of defeat in open war.
{type:narration} * Effect: The Igigi faction splits.
{type:narration} The majority flee via scatter protocol, while a minority stays to fight and die.
{type:narration} This act of "cowardice" triggers the Anunnaki's desire for punishment.
{type:narration} * Characters Affected: Zephyr, Pazuzu, Ishara, the entire Igigi race.
{type:narration} 2.
{type:narration} Event Name: The Punishment
{type:narration} * World Era / Calendar System: Shortly after The Scatter.
{type:narration} * Cause: The Anunnaki High Council, led by Enlil, decides to make an example of the escaped Igigi rather than pursue them directly.
{type:narration} * Effect: The punishment is not merely physical but psychological.
{type:narration} The war-moon Nibiru is moved into a permanent eclipse orbit around Sol-3, plunging the world into an endless, engineered winter.
{type:narration} As War-Matron Tiamat states, the goal is to give the Igigi "survival—just enough to make them regret the choice." This establishes the Anunnaki's defining trait as architects of "elegant cruelty" and makes the stakes emotional, not just physical.
{type:narration} * Characters Affected: Zephyr and the scattered Igigi on Sol-3, the native Tidecaller Giants, the primitive human populations.
{type:narration} 3.
{type:narration} Event Name: The First Impossible Friendship
{type:narration} * World Era / Calendar System: Year 14 of The Punishment.
{type:narration} * Cause: Zephyr, facing certain death in a super-blizzard, calls for help and is rescued by the Tidecaller Giant, Torrhen.
{type:narration} * Effect: Zephyr and Torrhen form a bond, leading to the synthesis of Igigi mathematics and Giant magic.
{type:narration} This is the birth of a new form of consciousness-crafting.
{type:narration} * Characters Affected: Zephyr, Torrhen.
{type:narration} 4.
{type:narration} Event Name: The Sacrifice & The Transformation
{type:narration} * World Era / Calendar System: Approximately 17 years after The Punishment began (three years into Zephyr's partnership with Torrhen).
{type:narration} * Cause: Nibiru's orbit destabilizes, threatening a biosphere-destroying cataclysm (a global flood) upon its departure.
{type:narration} * Effect: Torrhen sacrifices his individual consciousness, dissolving it into the planet's fundamental forces to protect Zephyr and encode their synthesized knowledge into the world itself.
{type:narration} The world is remade, and Zephyr is transformed into "the Sage."
{type:narration} * Characters Affected: Torrhen (is transformed), Zephyr (becomes the Sage).

{type:blank}
{type:narration} * Location / Region Name: Sol-3 (Earth)
{type:narration} * Type: Planet.
{type:narration} Initially a "primitive world" with a developing carbon-based biosphere and pre-technological indigenous populations.
