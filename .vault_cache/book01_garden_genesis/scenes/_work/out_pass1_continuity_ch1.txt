{type:narration} 2.0 Cosmological & Historical Canon

{type:narration} * The Primordial State: In the time before the cataclysm, two worlds existed in harmony: Mars, a planet of rivers and volcanic light, and Tiamat, a vast blue ocean world.

{type:narration} * The Cataclysm: The rogue god-planet Marduk appeared, its passage through the void described as a "furious sun." It first destroyed Mars, burning it to ash, and then collided with Tiamat, shattering the planet into a storm of dust and debris.

{type:narration} The Akashic Records, an ethereal library containing the memory of Tiamat, settled into orbit around Earth.
{type:narration} * The Long Vigil: Following the cataclysm, the six Aeon Keepers retreated into the Akashic Records to preserve the cosmic infrastructure.
{type:narration} They remained in the Ethereal Realm for a precisely recorded duration of 3,417 years, observing as Earth slowly reformed and became habitable.
{type:narration} * The First Coming: After approximately 3,500 years, Marduk returned.
{type:narration} Its reappearance is considered a "crimson omen of the next reckoning," establishing a future threat that looms over the saga.

{type:narration} 3.0 Principal Factions and Beings

{type:narration} The narrative of The Garden of Genesis is built upon the interactions of three core groups of sentient beings: the Aeon Keepers, the Neferati, and the Giants.
{type:narration} A precise understanding of their distinct origins, intrinsic natures, and complex interrelationships is critical for maintaining narrative integrity and respecting the established character motivations.

{type:narration} 3.1 The Aeon Keepers
{type:blank}
{type:narration} The Aeon Keepers are six ethereal consciousnesses who survived the destruction of Tiamat.
{type:narration} They are not physical beings but exist as distributed awareness within the Ethereal Realm, functioning as willing guardians of the cosmic infrastructure.
{type:narration} Their base of operations is the Akashic Records, a "library of pure knowledge" that orbits Earth.
{type:narration} From here, they monitor and maintain the flow of vrill between dimensions.

{type:narration} Theron	Temporal mechanics and timelines
{type:narration} Vaelith	Pragmatic analysis of the Veil
{type:narration} Mordain	Temporal archives of the Akashic Records
{type:narration} Korath	Bedrock, physical form parameters
{type:narration} Syreth	Memory of physical sensation

{type:narration} Key Limitation: The Aeon Keepers exist without physical form and cannot directly intervene in the physical world.
{type:narration} Their influence is limited to observation and sending faint, harmonic impressions through the vrill currents.
{type:narration} The "Prime connection" they establish with Senareth is the primary example of their indirect guidance, offering encouragement and clarification but never direct control.

{type:narration} 3.2 The Neferati
{type:blank}
{type:narration} The Neferati are a race of one thousand beings manifested directly by the Aeon Keepers from "thought-seeds" and vrill.
{type:narration} They were created to serve as ground-level intermediaries on the newly formed Earth.
{type:blank}
{type:narration} * Physical Characteristics: Humanoid in form, their bodies are composed of crystallized vrill, giving their skin a natural bioluminescence.
{type:narration} As newly manifested beings, they experience physical sensations like weight, temperature, and gravity with overwhelming and often disorienting novelty.
{type:narration} * Core Purpose: The Neferati were created to be autonomous guides for the Giants.
{type:narration} Their mission is to teach the Giants how to refine their innate abilities through cooperation, not to rule or control them.
{type:narration} The Aeon Keeper Lyaris explicitly crafted them with "curiosity" and "compassion" to facilitate this goal.
{type:narration} * Key Individuals:
{type:narration} * Senareth: Designated Prime upon manifestation, serving as the leader, coordinator, and primary conduit for the Aeon Keepers' faint impressions.
{type:narration} * Elyraen: Possesses a deeply analytical and observant nature, focused on data collection and understanding causality.
{type:narration} * Vairis: Embodies a practical and pragmatic worldview, focused on immediate needs like shelter and defense.
{type:narration} * Olythae: An artistic consciousness who uses the creation of patterns in sand and stone as a primary form of communication.
{type:narration} * Kyreth: A gentle and patient being who successfully made first contact with the Giant Torhh through the shared manipulation of vrill in water.
{type:narration} * Crucial Ancestral Distinction: The newly created Neferati must not be confused with their ancestors from Tiamat, the Nehereti.
{type:narration} The source texts establish that the Nehereti harassed and drove the ancient Pelagor from land.
{type:narration} This history is the source of the Giants' inherited trauma and is a canon fact of critical importance.

{type:narration} 3.3 The Giants (Transformed Pelagor)
{type:blank}
{type:narration} The Giants are the descendants of the Pelagor, a sentient, octopus-like species that inhabited the oceans of Tiamat.
{type:narration} Following the cataclysm, surviving Pelagor retreated into deep thermal vents, where they evolved over more than 3,000 years into their current form.
{type:blank}
{type:narration} * Physical Characteristics: Giants are massive beings, standing between 20 and 45 feet tall.
{type:narration} Their bodies are composed of living stone and crystallized minerals, granting them immense strength.
{type:narration} They possess powerful, albeit nascent and unrefined, geomantic abilities, allowing them to manipulate earth and water through consciousness.
{type:narration} * Geomantic Tribes: The Giants have diversified into distinct groups based on their elemental adaptations:
{type:narration} * Jade-Greens: Descended directly from the sea-born Pelagor; carry the deepest ancestral trauma. (Disposition: Watchful, traumatized, hesitant).
{type:narration} * Burnt-Reds: Adapted to desert environments, with bodies resembling weathered sandstone. (Disposition: Unafraid, curious, pragmatic).
{type:narration} * Charcoal-Greys: Composed of volcanic rock. (Disposition: Observant, fascinated by Neferati phenomena).
{type:narration} * Frost-Veined: Mountain-adapted Giants whose bodies are striated with patterns resembling snow and ice. (Disposition: Ancient, wise, open to parley).
{type:narration} * Copper-Greys: Associated with underground minerals and caves. (Disposition: Creative, engaged in collaborative art).
{type:narration} * Green-Browns: Forest-adapted beings whose forms incorporate moss and plant life. (Disposition: Creative, engaged in collaborative art).
{type:narration} * The Source of Fear (Canon Directive): The fear exhibited by the jade-green Giants toward the Neferati is the central conflict of Book 1.
{type:narration} It is an inherited, biological memory of being harassed, chased, and driven from the shores of Tiamat by the Neferati's ancestors, the Nehereti.
{type:narration} This trauma is not a misunderstanding; it is a historical fact encoded into their very being. (Canon Directive): All future narratives must treat this trauma not as a simple misunderstanding to be overcome, but as a foundational, biological truth for the jade-green Giants.
{type:narration} It is a legitimate historical wound, and its healing must be portrayed as a slow, complex process of earning trust, never as a quick fix.
{type:narration} To downplay this history is to violate the core conflict of the saga's foundational text.

{type:narration} 4.2 The Dimensional Structure
{type:blank}
{type:narration} The cosmos is composed of several distinct but interconnected realms and locations.
{type:blank}
{type:narration} * The Ethereal Realm: A higher dimension of pure consciousness, existing "between thought and form." It is the domain of the Aeon Keepers.
{type:narration} * The Physical Realm: The lower-frequency world of matter, weight, and linear time, where Earth is located.
{type:narration} * The Veil: The dimensional friction-space between the Ethereal and Physical realms.

{type:narration} * The Akashic Records: A vast, ethereal library that serves as the base of operations for the Aeon Keepers.
{type:narration} It orbits the Earth in the Ethereal Realm and contains the "memory of a world undone."

{type:narration} A Fragile Truce: Senareth's act of apology to the Giant Torhh has successfully cracked the wall of inherited fear, but it has not erased it.
{type:narration} Trust has been initiated but is far from being fully earned.
{type:narration} The historical trauma of the jade-green Giants remains a significant, unresolved tension.

{type:narration} The jade-green Giants, however, remain separate and watchful.
{type:narration} While Torhh has expressed a willingness to trust, their people as a whole are undecided and are still processing their ancestral trauma.

{type:narration} The Neferati Mission: The Neferati's original purpose of simple "guidance" has fundamentally shifted.

{type:narration} 6.0 Inviolable Thematic Pillars
{type:blank}
{type:narration} Beyond plot points and lore, the Sage Saga is defined by a set of core philosophical themes.
{type:narration} These themes are the narrative's soul and must remain central to all future storytelling to maintain the series' unique identity and emotional resonance.
{type:blank}
{type:narration} * Cooperation Over Conquest: The primary narrative engine of the saga is the building of alliances, not the winning of wars.
{type:narration} Progress is achieved through mutual understanding, shared creation, and partnership, as explicitly stated by the goal to lay the Garden's foundation on cooperation.
{type:narration} * The Weight of Memory and Trauma: History is not static backstory; it is an active, living force that shapes present-day fears, prejudices, and motivations.
{type:narration} The Giants' fear is the primary example of this, demonstrating that ancestral wounds must be acknowledged and addressed before true progress is possible.
{type:narration} * Creation Through Vulnerability: True progress and trust are achieved not through demonstrations of power, but through acts of vulnerability, patience, and apology.
{type:narration} Senareth kneeling before Torhh is the quintessential act of Book 1, proving that acknowledging harm is a greater strength than denying it.
